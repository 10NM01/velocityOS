Mission Statement - 
velocityOS is built on a privacy and zero artificial intellegence inititive. We believe that privacy is a right and continious monitoring through loopholes or pure blatency is both a violation of rights and laws but also extremely unethical. Thats why velocityOS is built purely around privacy but in a way you can use daily and doesnt feel like an extra stretch for something that should be enforced. We also belive artifical intellegence is a good thing but when doing crucial and important development developers should prioritize safety and security, and artificial intellegence is not the best in those types of projects. Sure humans might have errors but you cant replace them with something that poses more risk to some level. Thats why velocityOS is going to be made from the ground up with 100% human input and trusted open source programming rather than generative and chat-style artifical intellegence being used.
